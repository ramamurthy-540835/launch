Executive recommendation

Win one neighbourhood before launching four cities.

LunchBox should begin as a tightly controlled B2B2C pilot: secure 2–3 schools in one Chennai delivery cluster, earn parent trust, prove repeat ordering, and only then expand.

The commercial reality: at ₹39 per meal, paid acquisition for a single order is unlikely to work. Marketing must lead parents toward weekly plans and schools toward campus-wide partnerships.

LunchBox Market Launch & Google Ads Strategy
Launch readiness

Paid traffic begins only after trust and fulfilment are real.

Current application warning: school names, ratings, “2,000+ parents,” nutrition language and checkout behavior appear to be demo or placeholder content. Advertising those elements before verification creates trust, policy and compliance risk.

Product

  • Real onboarded schools
  • Working payment or confirmation
  • Usable “Request my school” form
  • Correct broken ₹ and punctuation characters
  • Mobile checkout fully tested

Trust

  • Real food photography
  • Verified testimonials only
  • Business address and support number
  • Delivery, cancellation and refund terms
  • Privacy policy and consent language

Food operations

  • Applicable FSSAI registration/license
  • Ingredients and allergens documented
  • Packaging and temperature SOPs
  • Batch traceability and recall process
  • Qualified review of nutrition claims

Measurement

  • Google Analytics and Ads tag
  • Order value captured
  • Campaign parameters retained
  • Test orders excluded
  • Weekly performance dashboard

Claim discipline

Use factual descriptions such as meal contents, price, participating school and delivery schedule. Do not advertise ratings, parent counts, nutritional benefits, “nutritionist planned,” “fresh,” or safety superiority until each claim is documented and defensible.

Food claims should be reviewed against FSSAI’s Advertising and Claims Regulations: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Compendium_Advertising_Claims_Regulations_04_10_2022.pdf

Readiness gate: no verified fulfilment, no paid launch.
Go-to-market model

Schools create access. Parents create recurring revenue.

1. Acquire the campus

Approach school owners, principals, administrators and parent committees with a limited pilot proposal. Provide tasting samples, menu details, operating procedures, delivery windows, escalation contacts and applicable food-safety documentation.

2. Activate parents through trusted channels

Give each participating school a dedicated landing page and QR code. Launch through the school’s official parent communication, then use WhatsApp reminders for weekly menus and cut-off times with appropriate consent.

3. Sell plans, not isolated meals

OfferIllustrative priceRole
One-meal trial₹39Remove first-purchase friction
Three-day plan₹117Flexible entry bundle
Five-day plan₹195Primary weekly conversion
Four-week plan₹780*Build predictable demand

*Illustrative, before holidays, fees or promotions. Final pricing must follow actual unit economics.

4. Build referrals after service quality is proven

Potential offer: when an existing and referred parent both complete a five-meal order, each receives one complimentary meal. Launch only after validating contribution margin and abuse controls.

Google Ads architecture

Start with high-intent Search, split by buyer.

Do not begin with Display, YouTube or Performance Max. Search gives tighter control while the business is learning which locations, schools and messages convert.

Initial settings

  • Google Search Network only; disable Display expansion.
  • Target only genuinely serviceable Chennai neighbourhoods.
  • Use “Presence” location targeting: people in the target area.
  • Run English and Tamil campaigns separately when both landing-page versions exist.
  • Test 6–9 AM and 6–11 PM, then optimize from conversion data.
  • Begin with Maximize Clicks and a cautious CPC ceiling; move to conversion bidding only after sufficient genuine conversion data.
  • Target adults and school decision-makers. Do not design personalized targeting toward children.

Lead qualification form

Collect school name, area, contact role, phone, email, approximate student count, current lunch arrangement, preferred meeting date and consent to contact. Do not collect student-level data in this form.

Sales follow-up

  1. Respond within one business hour during operating hours.
  2. Confirm serviceability and approximate volume.
  3. Schedule decision-maker discovery and tasting.
  4. Provide compliance pack and written pilot scope.
  5. Agree success criteria, support ownership and launch date.
Primary conversion: qualified school meeting. Secondary conversion: tasting booked.

Maximum acquisition cost
Gross profit per meal × expected meals per parent − fulfilment/support allowance − target profit.

Weekly decision dashboard

  • Spend, impressions, clicks and search terms
  • Completed orders and qualified school leads
  • Cost per first parent and cost per qualified school lead
  • Average order value and weekly-plan share
  • Four-week parent repeat rate
  • Cancellation, refund, late-delivery and support rates
  • Contribution margin after kitchen, packing, delivery and payment fees

Optimization rules

  • Review search terms twice weekly and add irrelevant queries as negatives.
  • Pause locations that cannot be served consistently.
  • Do not raise budgets while tracking, payment or delivery data is incomplete.
  • Scale only when incremental orders remain contribution-positive and repeat rate is healthy.

Google Ads conversion guidance: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16560108?hl=en

Measurement principle: a conversion is valid only when the order can be fulfilled.
